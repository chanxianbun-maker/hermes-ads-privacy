Privacy Notice for Hermes Amazon Ads

Last updated: October 12, 2026

Hermes Amazon Ads is an internal tool intended to help its operator review and improve the performance of Amazon advertising accounts that the operator owns or is authorized to manage. It is not offered as a public software service.

1. Information We Access

With an authorized user's permission, the tool may access Amazon Ads account identifiers, campaign and ad group settings, keyword and search term data, bids, budgets, impressions, clicks, spend, attributed sales, and other advertising performance metrics made available by Amazon Ads APIs.

The tool may also process authentication credentials and tokens needed to maintain authorized API access. We do not request Amazon account passwords through the tool.

2. How Information Is Used

We use this information only to analyze advertising performance, prepare internal reports, detect inefficiencies, and produce recommendations about budgets, bids, targeting, and campaign structure. Changes to advertising campaigns, if enabled in the future, will be governed by operator-defined approval and access controls.

3. AI and Service Providers

Advertising information may be processed using AI models or infrastructure service providers configured by the operator for analytics and recommendations. Only information needed for those functions should be shared, subject to applicable provider terms and security measures.

4. Data Storage and Security

We intend to limit access to authorized operators, protect API credentials, and use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards. Data may be stored on systems under the operator's control or processed by configured service providers. No security method can guarantee absolute protection.

5. Sharing and Sale of Data

We do not sell Amazon advertising data or offer it to unrelated advertisers. We may share information with technical service providers as needed to operate the tool, or when required by law.

6. Retention and Deletion

Data is retained only as reasonably needed for internal advertising analysis, recordkeeping, and security. Authorized operators may request deletion of locally held data, subject to legal obligations and technical limitations. Access to Amazon Ads data can be revoked through the applicable Amazon authorization process.

7. Changes to This Notice

We may update this notice when our data practices change. The latest version and its update date will appear on this page.
